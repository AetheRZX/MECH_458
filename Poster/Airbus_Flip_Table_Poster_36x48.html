Project Overview
Client: Airbus Robotics

Flip tables are large tabletops (~2.4 m × 1.8 m) that vertically translate and rotate. They simulate fuselage positions for robots used to drill or install fasteners on an aircraft fuselage at different orientations.

Problem

Current flip tables are manually rotated and difficult to operate, limiting throughput and introducing ergonomic risk for operators.

Objectives & Scope
  • Redesign the flip table to implement automated vertical and rotational motion
  • Integrate a safety system and Human-Machine Interface (HMI)
  • Improve ergonomic design and ease of access over and under the table
  • Achieve a minimum vertical speed of 0.05 m/s

*Rotation was downscoped after client discussion — a separate detailed rotation design will be provided.
